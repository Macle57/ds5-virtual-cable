ds5bridge bundled installer -- third-party notices
====================================================

The ds5bridge installer embeds, unmodified and byte-for-byte as published by
their authors, the official installers of two independent driver packages.
They are installed as separate products under their own licences; ds5bridge
neither modifies nor re-signs them. Their original copyright and licence
notices are reproduced below, as those licences require.

ds5bridge itself contains one compiled third-party library, SpeexDSP (its
echo canceller and resampler), in every build; its notices are the last
section below.

Neither project endorses ds5bridge. Support questions about the bundled
copies should go to the ds5bridge issue tracker, not to the upstream authors.
